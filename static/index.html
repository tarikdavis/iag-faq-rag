FAQ RAG demo

Ask a question. Choose an OpCo to apply the hard pre-retrieval filter. The pipeline retrieves top-k FAQ chunks, sends them to Claude with a grounded prompt, and returns a cited answer.
Why no knowledge graph here? The FAQ corpus has a flat relational structure (Hub → Topic → FAQ, plus one-hop relatedFaqs) that's fully covered by chunk metadata — a graph layer would add complexity without unlocking multi-hop traversal. If the schema grew richer (e.g. FAQ cites Policy → Policy applies in Market → Market has Tier benefits), a vector + graph hybrid would start to earn its keep.
